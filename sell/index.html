Questions people ask

How much is my El Paso home worth?

My free value tool gives an instant estimated range from your address. For a price you can list at, I pull recent sales of similar homes near you from the MLS and walk you through them on a call.

Do I have to fill out a seller's disclosure in Texas?

Most Texas home sellers must give buyers a seller's disclosure notice about the property's condition. I'll give you the TREC form and go through it with you.

Can I sell and buy at the same time?

Yes. We plan the timing of both so you're not stuck paying two mortgages or moving twice. Tell me which comes first for you.
