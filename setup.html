What Sofia does

Two separate jobs. They work independently.

  • She answers your phone — when you forward your line to her, she picks up every call you can't. She talks through what they're looking for, books them on your calendar, and texts you what happened.
  • She calls people from your website — the moment someone fills out your form, their phone rings in about 60 seconds. This one is already running. Nothing to set up.
  • She works in English and Spanish, day, night, and weekends.
  • She only books inside the hours you set, and she checks your calendar first so she never double-books you.
  • If someone doesn't answer, she texts them and tries again at 5 minutes, 30 minutes, and an hour.

Turn her off

Any time. Takes five seconds and your line goes straight back to normal.

T-Mobile / AT&T ##61# Tap to turn off
Verizon *73 Tap to turn off
On iPhone's menu, just toggle Call Forwarding off. Turning Sofia off does not stop her from calling your website leads — that keeps running.
