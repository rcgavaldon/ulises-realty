The homestead exemption: file it

If the home is your primary residence, Texas takes $140,000 off its value for school taxes. On a $250,000 home in the City of El Paso that's roughly $1,513 a year, every year. It's free to file with the El Paso Central Appraisal District, and plenty of homeowners never do.
