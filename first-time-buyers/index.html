Help with the down payment

Texas has statewide programs that help first-time and moderate-income buyers with the down payment and closing costs, through approved lenders:

  • TSAHC (Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation): Homes for Texas Heroes, for teachers, police, firefighters, EMS, veterans and other eligible professions, and Home Sweet Texas, for moderate-income buyers.
  • TDHCA (Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs): My First Texas Home, for first-time buyers.
  • City of El Paso: the city has offered down payment assistance to income-qualified first-time buyers through its community development programs.

Loan types many first-time buyers use: FHA (3.5% down with a 580+ credit score), conventional loans with as little as 3% down, and VA loans with no down payment for eligible veterans and service members (more on VA loans).

Programs, income limits and funding change. Your lender confirms what you qualify for.

Don't forget property taxes

Texas has no state income tax, so property taxes are higher than many buyers expect. A $250,000 home in the City of El Paso runs about $5,096 a year in property taxes with the homestead exemption, and the rate changes with the city and school district. Questions people ask

How much do I need to buy a house in El Paso?

It depends on the loan: FHA is 3.5% down, some conventional loans 3%, and VA loans 0% for eligible buyers. You'll also have closing costs, which down payment programs and seller concessions can help cover. A lender gives you exact numbers.

Do I need perfect credit?

No. FHA loans allow credit scores from 580 with 3.5% down. A lender can tell you where you stand and what would help.

What is the homestead exemption?

Once the home is your primary residence, Texas takes $140,000 off its value for school taxes. You file it once with the El Paso Central Appraisal District, and it's free.
