VA loans, in plain English

Eligible service members, veterans and some surviving spouses can buy with a VA-backed loan: no down payment and no private mortgage insurance, with a one-time VA funding fee that can be rolled into the loan (it's waived for many veterans with a service-connected disability). You'll need your Certificate of Eligibility, which a VA lender can pull for you.

Texas tax breaks for disabled veterans

  • 100% disabled (or rated unemployable) veterans can get a total exemption from property taxes on their homestead.
  • 10% to 90% disability ratings get a partial exemption of $5,000 to $12,000 off the home's taxable value.
  • Every homeowner living in the home also gets the $140,000 school homestead exemption.

You file these with the El Paso Central Appraisal District. Rules and amounts come from the Texas Tax Code; the appraisal district confirms what applies to you.

Questions people ask

Can I buy a house in El Paso before I PCS?

Yes. Many buyers shop from where they're stationed now, with online listings, a VA pre-approval and a local agent, and close around their report date. Tell me your timeline and I'll plan backward from it.

Do I need a down payment with a VA loan?

Not if you're eligible and the price is within what the lender approves. You'll still have some closing costs, which can often be negotiated with the seller.

Should I buy or rent when I get to Fort Bliss?

It depends on how long you expect to stay and what you'd pay in rent. I'll show you both side by side, taxes included.
